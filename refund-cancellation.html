Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

1. Order Cancellation

Because food orders are prepared specifically for customers, cancellation may not be possible after the restaurant has accepted or started preparing an order. Please contact us as soon as possible if you need assistance.

2. Payment Failure

If money is debited from a customer's account but the order is not successfully confirmed, the transaction will be checked against the payment provider/order records. Any eligible refund will be processed through the applicable payment channel.

3. Duplicate or Excess Payment

If a customer is charged more than once for the same order, the duplicate successful payment will be reviewed and, where confirmed, handled for refund through the applicable payment channel.

4. Missing or Incorrect Items

If an order has a missing or incorrect item, please contact us promptly with the order details so that the issue can be reviewed and an appropriate resolution can be provided.

5. Refund Method and Timing

Where a refund is approved, it will normally be returned through the payment channel used for the transaction. The time taken for the credit to appear can depend on the payment provider and the customer's bank.

6. Contact

For refund or cancellation assistance, please use the Contact Us page.
